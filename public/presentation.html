The 300 Seconds of Truth

Why survival is determined in the gap between the emergency call and the ambulance arrival.

The Biological Point of No Return

Ground-Level Sociology

Accidents Don't Wait for Adults

On school playgrounds and public streets, panic causes frozen inaction or dangerous home remedies.

1. The Bystander Paradox
When 20 people witness an accident, everyone assumes someone else already called 112, or pulls out a phone to record videos.
2. Playground Panic
Deep cuts, sprains, and asthma attacks happen in schoolyards and corridors before teachers can cross the field.
3. Dangerous Desi Remedies
80% of families panic: smearing toothpaste on burns, raw haldi in cuts, or iron keys in seizures, causing severe tissue sepsis.

Sanjeevani Blueprint (The Solution)
